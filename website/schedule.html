A conversation, not a pitch.

You will speak to Tim or Kim — the two people who underwrite and operate the properties. Plenty of these calls end with “not right now,” and that is a perfectly good outcome.

No slide deck No follow-up sequence Reschedule anytime

Yours first, ours second.

We start by asking what you are actually trying to accomplish — income now, growth over a decade, a tax problem this year, or getting out of being a landlord. The answer changes whether we are a fit at all.

  • What you are trying to build and over what horizon
  • Whether private real estate fits alongside what you already own
  • How our deals are structured — fees, the waterfall, the hold period
  • What could go wrong, and what happens if it does
  • Anything you want to ask about us, our record, or our debt policy

What we will and will not do.

What we will not do

  • Put you into an automated drip sequence
  • Call you repeatedly to “check in”
  • Pass your details to anyone else
  • Pressure you toward a deal that does not fit
  • Pretend a lock-up is shorter than it is

What we will do

  • Send anything we promised on the call
  • Tell you plainly if we are not a fit
  • Notify you when an offering opens, if you want that
  • Answer follow-up questions from your CPA or attorney
  • Leave you alone until you say otherwise
