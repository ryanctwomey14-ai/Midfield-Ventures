2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, email address and, if you provide it, phone number.
  • Investor profile — your accreditation status, and optionally your typical investment size and timing.
  • Correspondence — anything you send us by email, on a call, or through the investor portal.
  • Subscription and verification information — if you proceed to invest, the identity, tax and accreditation-verification information required by law and by our administrators.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages viewed, referring source, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type.
  • Cookies — see section 7.

3. How we use it

  • To send you the investor packet and other material you request.
  • To contact you about offerings, market notes and firm updates, where you have asked to hear from us.
  • To determine which material we are permitted to send you — your accreditation answer affects this directly, because securities rules limit what may be shared with non-accredited investors.
  • To administer your investment, including distributions, reporting and tax documents.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, anti-money-laundering and record-keeping obligations.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent, trade or otherwise transfer it to lead brokers, other sponsors, or any third party for their own marketing purposes.

We do not send your accreditation status, investment size or timing answers to advertising or analytics platforms.

5. Who we share it with

  • Service providers — email platform, CRM, investor portal, fund administrator, accreditation verification provider, accountants and attorneys. [List the actual providers once selected.]

  • Authorities, where required by law, regulation, subpoena or to protect our legal rights.
