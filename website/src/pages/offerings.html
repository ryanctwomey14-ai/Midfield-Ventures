The rules are the point.

Private offerings are sold under exemptions from securities registration. Those exemptions come with conditions, and one of them governs who may see deal terms and when.

A sponsor who publishes returns and property specifics on an open web page is either relying on an exemption that permits it — and verifying every investor properly — or is breaking the rules. It is worth knowing which, about anyone you invest with.
