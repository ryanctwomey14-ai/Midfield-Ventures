Are you accredited? Most professionals are, and don’t realise it.

The SEC definition is narrower than “wealthy” and broader than people assume.

You generally qualify if any one of these is true. You do not need all of them.

More on accreditation in the FAQ →

  • Income: over $200,000 individually, or $300,000 with a spouse, in each of the last two years — and you reasonably expect the same this year
  • Net worth: over $1,000,000, excluding the value of your primary residence
  • Licence: you hold an active Series 7, Series 65 or Series 82
  • Entity: you are investing through a trust or entity with over $5,000,000 in assets
