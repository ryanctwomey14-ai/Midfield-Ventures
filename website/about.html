About Midfield Ventures.

We are a husband-and-wife partnership that acquires underperforming apartment communities in emerging markets, improves them, and operates them for investors who want the ownership without the work. We invest alongside our investors in every deal we sponsor.

Buy well, improve honestly, pay partners consistently.

We identify and acquire high-potential multifamily assets and operate them for consistent, risk-adjusted returns.

Co-founder · Asset management & construction

Tim Dandini

Tim spent more than 30 years in commercial construction and project management before Midfield. He has scoped, bid, budgeted and delivered the exact kind of work a value-add apartment business plan depends on — roofs, mechanical systems, exteriors, unit interiors, and the unglamorous items that quietly destroy a renovation budget when nobody has priced them properly.

Why that matters to a limited partner: most sponsors buy construction expertise from a contractor after they own the building. Tim walks the property before we bid it. When he says a capital budget is realistic, that opinion was formed over three decades of being responsible for the answer.

At Midfield he leads asset management, the renovation programme and oversight of property management.

Co-founder · Underwriting & acquisitions

Kimberly Dandini

Kim is a U.S. military veteran who went on to spend 26 years in the technology industry. She retired. It did not take. Within months she had found the next thing worth doing and taken over acquisitions analysis for Midfield.

Why that matters to a limited partner: underwriting is not a sales function, and treating it like one is how sponsors talk themselves into bad deals. Kim builds and stress-tests the model on every property we consider — rent growth, exit cap rate, expense load, debt structure, what happens if the renovation runs long. On the large majority of properties we review, her answer is no. That is the job working correctly.

She also runs investor reporting, because the person who built the model should be the person explaining the results.
